# WhatsApp Booking — Names We Need

To place a booking on WhatsApp we need clear identity details. Pronouns alone
are not enough — phrases like “my child” or “our kid” do not tell us who to put
on the register.

### What to send
1. **Parent’s full name** (the adult on this WhatsApp number).
2. **Child’s full name** (the student joining the class).
3. **Which class** (e.g. Phonics, Math, Mandarin, Writing, Playgroup, or Trial).

### Example replies
- “I’m Alex Wong, child is Mia Wong, looking at Math.”
- “Parent: Sam Lee. Child: Jamie Lee. Phonics please.”

If any of these are missing, the assistant will ask again before offering seats.
We never invent a child name from “my”, “our”, or similar wording.
