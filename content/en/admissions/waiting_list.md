# Waiting List

If your preferred slot is full:
1. We place your child on the **waitlist** (usually moves within **1–3 weeks**).
2. You may start in the **nearest same-level group** so learning is not delayed.
3. When your preferred seat opens in the **same term**, we offer a **free transfer**.

Message us with the programme, preferred day/time, and child’s age — or book a Trial Assessment and we will advise the best available group.
