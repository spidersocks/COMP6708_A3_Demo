# Birthdays & Classroom Celebrations

- We are happy to **sing Happy Birthday** at the end of class if you tell the teacher that week.
- **No full parties** in lesson time — rooms turn over quickly for the next group.
- Store-bought nut-free cupcakes or stickers are welcome **only with 24 hours’ notice** and teacher approval (nut-aware centre).
- Please do not bring candles, balloons that pop loudly, or homemade food with unknown allergens.
- Party invitations may be handed out at pickup if every classmate is invited (or via parent WhatsApp with admin help).
