# Child Progress & Feedback

How parents usually hear about progress:

- **After each lesson:** a short verbal note at pickup (what went well + one home tip)
- **Every ~4 lessons:** a brief WhatsApp summary from the class teacher
- **Mid-term & end-of-term:** a short written progress note covering skills, participation, and suggested practice

If you need a **named / lesson-specific** update (e.g. “How did Emily do today?”), tell us the child’s name and lesson time — admin will pull the register and reply shortly. Meanwhile the cadence above is our standard for all groups.
