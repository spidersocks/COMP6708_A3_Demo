# Health, Allergy & Safeguarding

**Allergies**
- BrightNest is a **nut-aware** centre: please do not bring peanut/tree-nut snacks for sharing.
- List allergies on the enrolment form; we flag them on the class register for teachers.
- For severe allergies, please bring any prescribed emergency medication labelled with your child’s name.

**When to keep your child home**
- Fever **≥ 38°C**, vomiting, diarrhoea, or doctor-confirmed contagious illness.
- Return when fever-free for 24 hours without medicine. Sick makeups follow our absence policy (24 hours’ notice when possible).

**Safeguarding & privacy**
- CCTV covers corridors and common areas (not toilets).
- Classroom photos/videos only with **written parent consent**.
- Visitors sign in at reception; under-3 groups always have two adults on duty.
