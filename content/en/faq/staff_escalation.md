# When We Connect You With Our Team

BrightNest’s chat assistant can answer many programme, fee, hours, and booking questions from our knowledge base.

**Ask to speak with our team** when you want a human follow-up — for example:
- You want a real staff member / manager / director to call or message you
- You are upset about an experience and want someone to take ownership
- Something feels urgent for your child’s safety or wellbeing
- You need a refund, withdrawal, or other decision a bot cannot finalise alone

**What we say to parents:** we never mention internal admin lists or spreadsheets. We say we have let our team know, and that somebody will reach out during working hours (sooner if safety-related).

**What happens internally:** the assistant sends a direct notice to the centre lead (staff inbox), separate from quiet knowledge-gap research flags.

Example parent reply:
“I've let our team know about your request to speak with our team. Somebody will reach out to you during working hours.”
