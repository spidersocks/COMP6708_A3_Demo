# Registration Fee & Deposit

**Trial Assessment**
- HK$180 — credited toward the first package if you enrol within **14 days**.

**New enrolment**
- No separate “joining fee”. You pay the chosen **lesson package** + any materials fee.

**Deposit / hold a seat**
- To hold a popular slot before payment clears, we may ask for a **HK$500 seat deposit**, deducted from the first invoice. Forfeited if you cancel the hold after **72 hours** without enrolling.

**Assessment for placement (some English tracks)**
- Certain interview / advanced tracks quote a **HK$650** director assessment — only when that pathway is recommended (see Parent–Admin Q&A).
