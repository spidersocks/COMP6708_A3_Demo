# Fees Overview (Published Ranges)

Keywords: fees, tuition, packages

BrightNest publishes the following **guide ranges** (HKD). Exact quotes depend on level and package:

| Programme | Typical package | Guide fee |
|-----------|-----------------|-----------|
| Playgroup (18m–3y) | 8 sessions | HK$2,480–2,880 |
| Phonics (K1–P2) | 8 sessions | HK$3,200–3,680 |
| Mandarin (K2–P4) | 8 sessions | HK$2,960–3,360 |
| Creative Writing (P1–P6) | 8 sessions | HK$3,360–3,920 |
| Trial Assessment | 45 minutes | HK$180 |

Materials fee (workbooks / readers): usually HK$180–320 per term, charged separately.

Sibling discount: **8%** off the lower package when two siblings enrol in the same term.

Private 1:1 **guide fees**: English/Math HK$650–850 / 45 min; Mandarin HK$580–720 / 45 min; **5%** off for a prepaid pack of 8. Final quote after a short placement chat.
