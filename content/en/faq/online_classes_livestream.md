# Online Classes & Livestream

- Regular group lessons are **in-person only** at our Central centre — we do **not** livestream classrooms (privacy + teaching quality).
- **Online makeups** are not the default. In rare cases (extended illness, typhoon week), admin may offer a short Zoom catch-up with the teacher — asked case by case.
- Private 1:1 can occasionally move online if both teacher and family agree (same fee).
- We do not record lessons for later viewing unless a specific marketing consent form is signed.
