# When to Prefer Staff Follow-up (Demo Guidance)

BrightNest Learning Centre is a **fictional demo centre**. Prefer answering from published FAQs whenever possible.

Only soft-hand off to staff (with a helpful interim reply) for:
- Named, child-specific progress (“How did Emily do today?”)
- Personal medical/allergy instructions for one child
- “Please tell/remind the teacher…” relay requests
- Dated leave / cancel / reschedule that needs a human to edit the register

For those cases, reply helpfully, e.g.:
“Thanks — I’ve noted this for our admin team and a colleague will confirm on WhatsApp shortly. Meanwhile, our makeup policy allows two makeups per 8-lesson package when you give 24 hours’ notice.”

Do **not** reply with a bare silence token. Always give parents something useful.
