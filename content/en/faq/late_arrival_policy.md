# Arriving Late

- Please aim to arrive **5 minutes early**. Lessons start promptly so the group stays together.
- If you will be more than **10 minutes late**, message WhatsApp so we can settle your child quietly without stopping the whole group.
- After **15 minutes**, the teacher may continue without a full restart of warm-up games — your child still joins, but that portion is not made up separately.
- Chronic lateness: admin may suggest an earlier slot so drop-off is calmer.
- Late **pickup** rules are separate (see Drop-off & Pickup).
