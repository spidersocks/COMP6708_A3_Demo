# How Our Bilingual Classrooms Work

- **Phonics / English programmes:** Mainly **English**. Teachers add **Cantonese support** for K1–K2 settlers when needed.
- **Mandarin programmes:** **Mandarin immersion** in class; brief English/Cantonese clarification at the door if a child is stuck.
- **Creative Writing:** English or Chinese stream depending on the enrolled programme.
- **Playgroup:** Warm bilingual talk (English + Cantonese) with songs and routines in both languages.

We are not a full dual-immersion international school — we are a learning centre that helps HK children build confidence across languages.
