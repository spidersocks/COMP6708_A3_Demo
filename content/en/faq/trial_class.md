# Trial / Assessment Path

Q: Do you offer a free trial class?  
A: We do **not** offer a free taster. Instead we recommend a paid **Trial Assessment** so teachers can place your child accurately.

**Trial Assessment**
- **45 minutes** · **HK$180**
- Covers language/level observation + brief parent feedback
- Fee is **credited** toward the first package if you enrol within **14 days**

**First regular lesson**
- Charged as a normal session once enrolled
- Teachers observe fit and confirm the group level

Ask “show trial availability” in this chat to see open assessment slots, or tell us your preferred day.
