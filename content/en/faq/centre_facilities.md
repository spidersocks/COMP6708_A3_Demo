# Centre Facilities

**Washrooms**
- Children’s toilets on **2/F** next to the playgroup room; adults’ washroom by reception.
- **Nappy / diaper changing** shelf available in the playgroup washroom — please use disposable liners and dispose of nappies in the sealed bin.

**Waiting area**
- Seats opposite reception; **free Wi‑Fi** (network name posted at the desk); drinking water dispenser.
- Quiet phone calls preferred — headphones for video calls if possible.

**Accessibility**
- Passenger lifts from the Harbourview Plaza lobby to 2/F. Strollers welcome in the lobby; fold larger strollers before classroom doors.

**Snacks**
- Water and simple snacks for playgroup only as advised by teachers. Please follow our **nut-aware** allergy policy.
