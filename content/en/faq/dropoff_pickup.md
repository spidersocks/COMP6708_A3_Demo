# Drop-off, Pickup & Waiting

- **Playgroup (under ~3):** One parent/carer may stay in the room for settling-in weeks as agreed with the teacher.
- **Other programmes:** Parents usually wait outside the classroom so children can focus.
- **Waiting area:** Seats opposite reception on 2/F; free Wi‑Fi; drinking water available.
- **Pickup:** Please arrive from **5 minutes before** the lesson ends. Teachers dismiss from the classroom door.
- **Late pickup:** We can supervise until **18:15 on weekdays** and **16:15 on Saturdays**. After that a late-care note may apply (HK$50 per 15 minutes) — rare; please message us if delayed.
- Siblings waiting: welcome in the lounge if supervised by a parent.
- **Helpers / grandparents:** may drop off and pick up when listed as authorized collectors (see Authorized Pickup FAQ).
