# Class Observation & Visits

**Regular lessons**
- To protect focus and privacy, parents and grandparents **do not sit inside** regular Phonics / Mandarin / Writing / Math rooms after settling-in.
- You are welcome to wait in the **2/F lounge** and chat briefly with the teacher at dismissal.

**Playgroup**
- One parent/carer may stay in-room during early settling weeks (see Drop-off & Pickup).

**Want to see teaching style first?**
- Book a **Trial Assessment** (45 min, HK$180) — that is our structured way to meet a teacher and see the approach.
- Open-house style observation slots are occasionally offered in **term-start week**; ask admin for the next date.

**Grandparents / visitors**
- Visitors sign in at reception. Classroom entry still follows the policy above unless staff invite you for a short hello at the door.
