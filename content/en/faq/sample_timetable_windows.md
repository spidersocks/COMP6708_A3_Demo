# Typical Class Time Windows

These are **guide windows** (exact seats confirmed after assessment / booking):

| Programme | Typical windows |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Playgroup | Mon–Fri mornings 09:30–11:30 |
| Phonics | Weekday afternoons 16:00–18:00; Sat 09:00–13:00 |
| Mandarin | Weekday afternoons 16:00–18:00; Sat 09:00–13:00 |
| Creative Writing | Sat mornings 09:30–12:30; some Thu 16:30–17:30 |
| Trial Assessment | By appointment Mon–Sat within opening hours |

We are open **Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00** and **Sat 09:00–16:00** (closed Sun & HK public holidays).

Ask “show trial availability” in this demo to see live bookable slots, or tell us your preferred weekday and we will suggest the nearest open seat.
