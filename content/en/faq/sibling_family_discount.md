# Sibling & Family Discount

**Sibling discount**
- When **two siblings** enrol in the **same term**, we offer **8% off the lower-priced package**.
- Example: Phonics HK$3,400 + Mandarin HK$3,100 → 8% off the Mandarin package.

**Three or more children**
- Ask admin for a custom family quote (usually an additional courtesy discount on the third package).

**What it does not cover**
- Trial Assessment fees, materials fees, and private 1:1 packs are **not** discounted under the sibling rate (private packs have their own prepaid 5% offer — see Fees Overview).

**How to claim**
- Tell us both children’s names when you enrol or renew. Admin applies the discount on the invoice.
