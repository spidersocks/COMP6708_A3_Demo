# Holiday & Term-Break Hours

- **Sundays & gazetted HK public holidays:** Closed.
- **Christmas Eve:** Open **09:00–13:00** (half day).
- **Christmas Day & Boxing Day:** Closed.
- **Chinese New Year:** Closed on gazetted CNY holidays (usually first three lunar days).
- **Summer / term breaks:** Regular weekly classes pause; optional short holiday workshops are announced on WhatsApp.
- Missed lessons during gazetted holidays can use the **makeup quota** in the first two weeks of the next teaching block when seats allow.
