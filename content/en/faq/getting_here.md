# Getting Here

**Address:** 2/F, Harbourview Plaza, 88 Queen's Road Central, Central, Hong Kong

**MTR:** Central Station **Exit D2**, about a **3-minute walk** along Queen’s Road Central. Look for the Harbourview Plaza lobby entrance.

**Lift / access:** Take the passenger lifts in the lobby to **2/F**. The centre entrance is opposite the lifts. Strollers are welcome in the lobby; please fold larger strollers before entering classrooms.

**Parking:** Paid public car park in Harbourview Plaza (hourly). We do **not** provide parking validation. Street parking nearby is limited — MTR is usually faster at peak hours.

**Visitors:** Please sign in at reception. A staff member will greet you for trials and first visits.
