# Authorized Pickup (Helper / Family)

**Who may collect your child**
- Parents/guardians listed on the enrolment form are always authorized.
- A **domestic helper**, grandparent, or other carer may collect **only if** they are named on our pickup list.

**How to authorize**
1. Message admin on WhatsApp with the collector’s **full name** and relationship (e.g. helper Ah Mei).
2. Or add them on the enrolment form under “Authorized collectors”.
3. On the first visit we may ask the collector to show **HKID / passport** at reception.

**Same-day changes**
- Please message us **before class ends**. We will not release a child to someone who is not on the list.

**Playgroup / under-3**
- Helpers are welcome for drop-off and pickup. Settling-in stays follow our drop-off policy.
