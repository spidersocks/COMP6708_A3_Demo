# What BrightNest Bot Can Help With

Keywords: demo, features, booking, availability, courses, RAG

BrightNest Learning Centre's assistant can help parents with:

- Course overviews (Phonics, Mandarin, Creative Writing, Playgroup)
- Typical fees and materials (published ranges only)
- Opening hours and severe-weather guidance
- Enrolment steps and trial assessment booking
- Live availability for trial and regular class slots
- Confirming a provisional booking (demo) without payment

For child-specific placement advice, we still recommend a short trial assessment with our teachers.
