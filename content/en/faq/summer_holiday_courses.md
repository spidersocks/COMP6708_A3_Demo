# Summer / Holiday Intensives

- Each July–August we run **short intensives** (usually 4–8 sessions) in Phonics brush-up, Mandarin conversation, and Creative Writing.
- Typical format: **4–5 mornings or afternoons per week** for 1–2 weeks, or once-weekly across the summer.
- Guide fees: about **HK$1,680–3,200** depending on length and programme (materials extra).
- Enrolment usually opens in **May**. Message us with your child’s age and preferred programme for the current summer timetable.
- Regular term packages do **not** automatically convert to summer intensives — ask admin if you want to transfer remaining lessons.
