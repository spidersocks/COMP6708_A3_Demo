# Dress Code (No Uniform)

- BrightNest **does not require a uniform**.
- Comfortable play clothes and **closed-toe shoes** work best (especially playgroup and phonics tracing).
- Please label jackets and water bottles.
- Avoid costumes or flip-flops on lesson days — stairs and active games are safer in trainers.
- Art / writing days: an old T-shirt is fine; we provide aprons for messy crafts.
