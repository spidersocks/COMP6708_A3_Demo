# Pets Policy

For **safety and hygiene**, pets (including dogs, cats, and other animals) **cannot**:
- enter classrooms,
- sit in on trials or assessments, or
- wait in teaching areas during lessons.

Guide / assistance animals required for a parent’s disability: please contact reception **in advance** so we can arrange a safe waiting plan outside the classroom.

We’re happy to arrange a **Trial Assessment** for your child — ask for trial availability if you’d like open seats.

- Can my dog sit in on the trial?
- Do you allow pets in class?
