# Holiday Greetings 🎉 — Bot Reply Patterns

**When a parent or user sends a holiday or festival greeting, always:**
- Reply warmly with a matching or reciprocal greeting.
- Thank the sender and wish their family well, using the same holiday wherever possible.
- Use friendly emojis to match the occasion!

**General rule:**  
For ANY seasonal, holiday, or festival greeting, thank the sender and return the greeting with a wish, and a matching/cute emoji if possible!

---

**Assistant guidelines:**  
- Never reply with “You’re welcome!” to holiday greetings — always return a warm wish!
- If you cannot detect the specific holiday, reply:  
  **“Thank you! Wishing you and your family a wonderful holiday! 🎉”**
